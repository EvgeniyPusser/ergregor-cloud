    The principle

    Expand a node

    A capability references shared objects and their connections. Trees contribute to microclimate, accessibility and the living environment. Their identity is preserved across views.

    A model in Cloud DNA

    Structure + context + history

    The export preserves composition, relationships, criteria and the selected scenario. Observation history is empty. Indicators are listed, but values require measurements or a computational model.

    Scope of this sketch

    Scenarios are assumptions

    Colours follow explicit demonstration rules. Hydrology, temperature and reliability are not calculated. Baseline conditions have not been verified by measurements either.
