Measurements, not claims

Heat & tests

Every number a model carries has to come from somewhere. This page is where they come from. A reduced thermal bench, checked against an independently validated reference, then run on 40 donor dwellings, 200 receivers and 21 measured climates from Stockholm to Beer Sheva.

The question behind all of it is not about walls. It is whether a technical object can accumulate operating experience and pass it on — and if so, what exactly travels. Buildings are the test case, because they are the best-documented one.

The bench, and what it is answerable to

A single zone with three thermal capacities — air with furniture, the envelope, internal partitions. Within each 15-minute step the system is linear and the state is advanced by matrix exponential, so there is no integration-step artefact by construction. Heating is an air-source heat pump whose available output falls with outdoor temperature, which is what turns a cold spell into a capacity problem rather than only a cost one.

It is calibrated against BuilDa, the open Modelica generator from Rosenheim, itself validated against ASHRAE 140-2004. Two correction factors were fitted on eight buildings in Munich and applied unchanged to four held out in Berlin, in another month, at other U-values:

1.016mean heat-demand ratio, fitting set
1.020same, held out
0.22 Kworst mean indoor temperature error, all twelve
≈ 1 sone heating season for 300 dwellings

The calibration says the physics agrees with an independent reference. It does not say the generated population resembles any real building stock. That is a separate claim and it is not made.

What travels between buildings

Three things were tried as the inherited object, in this order: a control policy, a finished body, and a rule. Each was given to all 200 receivers from all 40 donors — every ordered pair evaluated, so the oracle rows below are exact, not estimates.

Across the first four rows the answer was the same and it was no. Similarity between donor and receiver predicted nothing: over all 8 000 ordered pairs the correlation of transfer gain with distance in ten standardised building parameters was +0.013, and with a physically motivated resilience trait −0.021. Permuting whole receivers gives p = 0.82 and p = 0.36. Picking the nearest donor by either measure was worse than picking one at random.

The reason was visible in the same matrix. Donors agreed with each other at +0.94: a donor was good or bad for every receiver rather than for some. Quality lay on a single axis, and that axis was insulation — correlation +0.90. When there is one axis there is nothing to match on.

Where matching does start to pay

The prediction was made before the run: give quality a second axis and matching should begin to earn its keep. Summer supplies that axis — thick walls and tight windows help in frost and hurt in heat. With Israeli weather records added against Nordic ones, the sign flipped:

+34 %the same mistake the other way round

Inside each half there is still nothing to match: concordance stays at 0.92 and 0.97 and perfect matching is worth 0 % and 2 %. The entire gain appears only across the boundary between two different problems.

Inheriting experience pays exactly where the receivers face different problems, and not where they face one problem of differing severity. Munich and Stockholm are one problem, differently hard. Israel and Sweden are two.

A correction to our own earlier recommendation. Between-donor concordance — which we proposed as the cheap pre-test — does not catch this: it stayed at 0.94 both before and after. The interaction share does: 6 % against 39 %. That is the number the model field applies.interaction now carries.

Where a simple rule exists, and where it does not

400 bodies were run in four climates, and the quality of each body was regressed on its own properties. How much is explained by properties taken one at a time:

ClimateExplained by single propertiesReading
Stockholm0.958a simple rule exists: insulate
Munich0.651mostly simple
Beer Sheva0.452combinations start to matter
Tel Aviv0.253no simple rule

In the north a single number carries the answer. In the south it does not, and the strongest pair found was window share × air-change rate — many windows help only if there is a way to flush the heat at night. Which is also why shutters are inherited separately from walls: in the north the body decides, in the south the daily routine does.

How much to insulate, once money is counted

With comfort as the only measure, the optimum runs to the edge of the range and the recipe becomes "insulate to the limit" — which is not a recipe. Adding construction cost (IWU Darmstadt, EU cost-optimal methodology, 30 years, 3 % discount) puts the optimum inside:

Insulating to the limit costs more than not insulating at all. And the order of what matters is the same in every climate zone — window, wall, floor, roof — only the amounts differ. What changes is the ratio: in warm zones the window outweighs the wall 2.8 times, in cold zones 1.6.

What this bench does not see

Every rule quietly assumes that the processes nobody named behaved the same way at both ends. Ours are named here rather than discovered later.

  • Shutters and any shading. Absent from the model entirely. In Israel they are closed every day on every window. Our hot-climate numbers are therefore overstated, and the "window share × air-change rate" pair stands in for a lever that real buildings operate differently.
  • Roof reflectance. The roof is one U-value. In a hot climate a white roof can outweigh centimetres of insulation.
  • Moisture and vapour transport. Not modelled.
  • Phase-change materials, evaporative cooling. Not modelled.
  • The price of airtightness and of heavy construction. No cost data was available, so those two were left out of the cost optimisation and stated as fixed.
  • Weather years. 32 city-years across 21 locations is a wide climate range but few statistically independent years. The Israeli climates have one year each.
  • The population is generated, from TABULA parameter ranges, not sampled from a register.

What this changes in the models

Four fields on every model record exist because of the runs above. They are described in MODELS.md and filled in for Heat: finding where it actually goes.

rules
What sits in the cloud is a rule, not a copy. A number written without its condition turns harmful the moment it crosses a climate — measured at +70 %.
applies
Every rule carries its reach as a number. Below 10 % one rule fits everyone; above 30 % the rule is addressed and the wrong address is expensive.
blindspots
What was not measured while the rule was derived, stated by the author rather than found by the next person.
weighting
The model lists what to measure and carries no weights. Part is computed from your site, part you declare — horizon, the price of discomfort, the view from the window. Taste enters on the same footing as climate.

The portal does not yet compute the interaction share itself: the number comes from an experiment and is typed in. Computing it automatically needs the bench to run inside the portal, against a population. That is the next piece of work.
