    Cloud Lab

    The minimum viable laboratory

    How the first online lab works

    1. A visitor opens a model and sees its current genome.
    2. The visitor studies linked systems, images, and source notes.
    3. The visitor adds a hypothesis, contact, or new branch idea.
    4. The cloud preserves the contribution as part of model history.
    5. Future AI layers connect similar thoughts across the ecosystem.

    Platform rules to support autonomous growth

    • Every contribution must point to a model, system, or question.
    • Every branch keeps author trace and a visible evolution log.
    • Teams can build their own track without asking permission first.
    • Shared standards connect separate efforts into one genome.
